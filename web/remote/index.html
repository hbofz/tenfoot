Cast from your phone's YouTube app

Works from anywhere, no shared Wi-Fi needed. Link your phone once:

  1. On the TV, open YouTube, then Settings → Link with TV code.
  2. On your phone, in the YouTube app, tap Cast → Link with TV code and type the code.

After that the Cast button plays and queues videos on the TV, like a Chromecast.
